Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and what we don't.

Plain language. No legal theatre. Last updated 2026-05-04.

What we collect.

When you submit your email through the diagnostic or the email capture, we store: your name (if provided), your email address, the source of capture, and your dominant interference pattern (if you ran the diagnostic). We also store transactional data for any purchases — tier purchased, date, Stripe customer ID, payment status.

We do not store credit card numbers. Payments are processed through Stripe. We never see the card data.

Why we collect it.

Email address: to send you the transmissions you signed up for and to deliver any products you have purchased. Diagnostic result: to send you the pattern-specific framework relevant to your result. Payment data: to grant access to the tier you purchased and to provide a record of the transaction.

Who we share it with.

Resend (transactional email delivery). Stripe (payment processing). Supabase (our database, hosted in the United States). Vercel (web hosting and analytics). Cloudflare (DNS and edge). That is the full list.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. We do not run third-party advertising trackers.

How long we keep it.

Subscriber data: as long as you remain subscribed. If you unsubscribe, your record is marked as unsubscribed and excluded from all future sends. Transactional purchase records: indefinitely, for tax and access-grant purposes.

Your rights.

You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email or the unsubscribe page. You can request a complete copy of your stored data, or full deletion, by emailing ace@sovereign-synthesis.com.
