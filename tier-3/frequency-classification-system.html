Tier 3 · Navigator · Module 02

Frequency Classification
System.

Four frequencies. Every room runs on one of them. Once you can name which, the room stops being a mystery and becomes architecture you can decide what to do with.

What this installs.

Module 01 surfaced what a room optimizes for. Module 02 names the frequency that produces that optimization. Every room runs on one of four — Fear, Ego, Attention, or Sovereign. Most run on the first three. The fourth is rare. After enough reads, the frequency registers in seconds, before words form for it.

The four frequencies.

Read each profile. Recognition is the first install. After you've read them, the classifier below is where you practice naming the frequency on a specific room.

Frequency 01

Fear. Survival Oscillation.

The room runs on threat. Survival logic dominates every decision.

Signals
  • Decisions are made against worst-case outcomes, not for best-case ones.
  • "What if it goes wrong" surfaces more than "what if it goes right."
  • Threat signals — job loss, social rejection, financial collapse — drive most behavior.
  • Energy stays defensive. Not a single net-new move comes out of the room.
Frequency 02

Ego. Validation Loop.

The room runs on external validation. Worth is leased from the audience.

Signals
  • People in the room are positioning more than producing.
  • Self-worth hinges visibly on external scoreboards — titles, follower counts, applause.
  • Comparison and ranking are constant background processes.
  • Energy in the room depends entirely on who is watching.
Frequency 03

Attention. Attention Fracture.

The room runs on fragmentation. Bandwidth is shredded; nothing finishes.

Signals
  • Conversations jump tracks before they resolve.
  • Notifications, interruptions, and multi-channel chatter saturate the air.
  • Outputs are shallow, fragmented, and immediately forgotten.
  • The longer you stay, the less you have to show for it.
Frequency 04

Sovereign. The Creative Plane.

The room runs on generation. Energy compounds; output is real.

Signals
  • The room rewards generation — making something that did not exist before.
  • Decisions come from clarity, not urgency.
  • Concepts and conversations resolve. Things complete.
  • The longer you stay, the more energy you have on exit, not less.

Classify a room.

Pick a specific room you have been in or are in now. Check the signals below that match. The frequency with the most matches is the operating frequency of the room.

Sovereign Tip

Classification installs at the level the room cannot touch. Once you can name the operating frequency of a room before the room captures you, you stop performing for rooms whose frequency caps your transmission. The Validation Loop room no longer pulls you into positioning. The Survival Oscillation room no longer drops you into reactivity. The Attention Fracture room no longer shreds your bandwidth.

This is the architecture of refusing capture. You enter a room knowing what its frequency is — and engagement becomes a choice you make consciously, not a default the room makes for you.

How to install Module 02.

Run this alongside the Environmental Read Protocol. They compound — the read names what a room optimizes for, and the classifier names which frequency produces that optimization.

Day 1

Read all four profiles above. Run the classifier on the room from your Module 01 Day 1 read. Name the frequency.

Days 2–6

Classify one room per day. Compare classifications across rooms. Notice when the same room shifts frequency depending on who is in it or what is at stake.

Day 7

Re-classify the rooms you've been in this week. Module 02 is installed when the frequency registers in seconds, before words form for it.
