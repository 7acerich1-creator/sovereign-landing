Navigator.

Where you are in the install. What is blocking the next phase. What to do next, with no guesswork. Run it weekly until the install completes — then run it monthly to keep the perimeter of conscious engagement intact.

What this installs.

The Navigator install is not linear in time — it is layered in capacity. This module runs the assessment, names the phase you are operating in right now, and surfaces the single next move that takes you into the phase above. No syllabus pacing. No arbitrary timelines. The phase is what your operation actually shows, not what week you are on.

The four phases.

Every Navigator install passes through these in order. Read each one. After the assessment below, your current phase is the highest one your operation actually meets.

Phase 01 · Reading

You can read a room with effort.

The Environmental Read Protocol works. Naming what a room optimizes for takes a minute or two of conscious attention. The read is real, but it costs energy.

Phase 02 · Classifying

The frequency registers.

Reading is faster. Naming the operating frequency — Fear, Ego, Attention, Sovereign — happens in seconds. The classification stops requiring the tool because the pattern is internalized.

Phase 03 · Axiom-driven

The deliberation drops away.

The three axioms operate ahead of the deliberation. You don't ask "is this room okay" — you ask "what frequency" because the first question is no longer a question. Categories of decision retire.

Phase 04 · Conscious engagement

You choose your rooms.

Entry is deliberate. You stop drifting into rooms by inertia. You leave rooms whose frequency caps your transmission. The Navigator is installed; from here it is maintenance, not learning.

Run the assessment.

Six questions. Yes or no, honestly. The phase is not aspirational — it is what your operation actually shows in the past two weeks.

Sovereign Tip

The Phase Navigator installs the practice of measuring progress by what your operation actually shows, not by how the work feels. The architect at Phase 03 is sovereign in their reading and their refusing. The architect at Phase 04 is choosing their rooms by frequency, not by inertia.

This is how you stop guessing whether you are doing the work right. The Navigator names where you are and what the next move is. Progress becomes self-evident in the operation — the install is visible from inside it.

01. Can you name what a room is optimizing for after a few minutes of attention?
02. Does the read happen automatically — under sixty seconds, no friction?
03. Can you name the operating frequency of a room without opening the classifier?
04. Do you find yourself running the three axioms without summoning them?
05. Have you left a room — relationship, role, group, conversation — in the past month because of its frequency?
06. Are you choosing your rooms now based on what you want to transmit, not on what is available by default?
Answer the six questions. Your current phase and next move appear here.
