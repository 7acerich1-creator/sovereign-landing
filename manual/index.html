Phase 1 · System Integrity Scan

The Diagnostic.

Identify the active scripts running in your operating system.

Four comparisons. Each one names a script. On one side is the default operating mode — the script most consciousness runs without choosing. On the other is the Architect mode — the script that has to be installed deliberately.

Click the side you recognize yourself in. Be ruthlessly honest. You cannot patch a bug you refuse to acknowledge.

NPC vs Architect. Used here as shorthand for two operating modes — not as an insult. NPC means "running the default script that came with the environment." Architect means "running a script you wrote yourself, with full awareness of the source code."

01 · The Input/Output Ratio

How does your day begin?

NPC · The Consumer

You absorb first.

You wake up and immediately check your phone. You absorb the world's emotion, anxiety, and noise before you have your own thoughts. The frequency is set by whatever notification arrived first.

Architect · The Player

You broadcast first.

You wake up and broadcast intention. You write, create, or visualize before any input. The frequency is set by you, not by the world.

02 · The Shadow Response

What happens when you feel a hard emotion?

NPC · The Victim

You look outward for cause.

When you feel anger or jealousy, you look for who caused it. You say "they made me feel..." The locus of control sits outside your operating system.

Architect · The Sovereign

You look inward for code.

When you feel a trigger, you look inward. You ask, "What part of my code is this revealing?" The trigger becomes diagnostic data instead of an indictment of someone else.

03 · The Time Dilation

What zone are you operating in?

NPC · The Prisoner

Survival Zone.

You are constantly trying to catch up. Time moves at the speed of stress. The next deadline is the only future you can see clearly.

Architect · The Traveler

Sovereign Zone.

You operate from a different clock. You know that high-frequency action collapses time. You don't hurry. You accelerate.

04 · The Reality Render

How do you hold your goals?

Hope, conditional.

You view your goals (money, freedom, work that matters) as things you hope will happen if conditions align. The goal lives somewhere outside you, waiting.
