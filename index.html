Why a formation, not a movement.

Every prior attempt to step out of the legacy operating system has been absorbed back into it. The radical to a holiday and a soundbite. The field has a known response for individual architects. It does not have a known response for a coordinated network operating from outside the frequency simultaneously.

That is the structural difference this time. The work below is how the network forms — one node at a time, one architecture installed at a time, until the formation is at scale.
