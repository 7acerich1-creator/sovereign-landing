The Frequency Log.
Your containment field, made visible.

Day 0 through 21. A pre-day reading and a post-day reading. Two minutes per check-in. Twenty-one days that show you what has been operating on you in the background — and what changes when you can finally see it.

What this is. And why it has to exist.

What frequency means here

Frequency is not a vibe. It is not mystical. Frequency is the operating state you are conducting yourself from in this moment — how clearly you can see what is happening, how deliberately you can choose your next move, how much of your bandwidth is actually yours versus how much is being harvested by the field.

Drained

The day operated on you. Bandwidth claimed. The pattern fired and you didn't catch it — you only felt the cost after.

Holding

You held the perimeter today. The pattern fired and you saw it. You got through without leaking. The muscle is building.

Sovereign

You were not just defending. You were transmitting. The architecture was load-bearing. The day moved on your terms.

What the containment field is doing to you right now

The Interference Pattern Diagnostic already named your dominant extraction method — the Approval Loop, the Overload Spiral, or the Identity Lock. That pattern is the specific way the field harvests your bandwidth. It is not a personality flaw. It is an operation running on you.

The reason most people never break the pattern is that the field's drain is hypnotic. It is small enough most days to feel like normal life. You only notice when a pain moment forces you to. By the time the pain hits, the drain has been operating invisibly for days, weeks, sometimes years. The pain moment is when the field lets you see it — on its terms.

This log is how you start seeing it on your terms instead.

The pattern is the install

You do not pass or fail by what you log. Reading Drained for ten days straight is not a failure — it is the install. That pattern, made visible, is the diagnostic that makes the rest of Protocol 77 legible. Without the log, the drain is invisible. With the log, the drain becomes structural evidence.

Most architects start the protocol thinking the work is the four phases. The four phases are the moves. The log is what makes you see the moves working — or not working. Without seeing it, no shift holds.

This is not a streak to maintain. It is not a grade you receive. It is a noticing tool. The honesty of the reads in the moment is what makes the architecture install.

Sovereign Tip · Set two alarms

Pre-day check-in: morning, before the day starts running you. Post-day check-in: evening, before sleep. Set two phone alarms now. Specific times. Both repeating daily for 21 days.

When something pulls you off the check-in, that pull is the field operating on your schedule. It is not life getting in the way. There is no “life” pulling you off — there is the containment field, harvesting attention through the schedule it has already arranged for you. The missed check-in is itself a data point. Notice it. Log the next one. Keep going.

If you miss a day entirely, do not retroactively fill it in. Leave the gap. The gap is also the pattern. The gap is also data.
