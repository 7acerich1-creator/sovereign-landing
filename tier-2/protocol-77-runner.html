Counter-Protocols for Biological Drag.

Biological drag is the failure-mode catalog for the four phases. When you feel pulled off-mission, identify which phase is being eroded — then deploy the matching counter-protocol below.

The Provocation Cascade Defends Phase 1 · Gray Rock ▾
Pattern An interference vector senses Gray Rock and escalates. First a small jab. Then a louder one. Then something explicit, designed to force a reaction. The cascade is structured to find the threshold where Gray Rock breaks.

Counter-Protocol Hold the flatline through the second and third escalation. Most architects break on round three. Round three is where the vector exhausts its arsenal — if you do not react, the pattern collapses and the cascade itself becomes evidence of the architecture you have just exposed.
The Re-Infection Vector Defends Phase 2 · Faraday ▾
Pattern A Noise stream you successfully embargoed sneaks back in through a "harmless" or "necessary" channel. A friend forwards a clip. The algorithm slips one past. You check "just one notification." The seal looks intact — but the signal is leaking.

Counter-Protocol Re-classify the channel that let it through. Tighten the embargo on that specific path. The leak is never the noise itself; the leak is the channel that decided to admit it. Treat the channel as the failure point, not the content.
The Crowd Pull Defends Phase 3 · Lighthouse ▾
Pattern Social pressure to leave the elevated position and "engage." Debate the wrong frame. Defend yourself against accusations made in bad faith. Justify your stance to people who do not have the prerequisites to evaluate it. The pull always feels reasonable in the moment.

Counter-Protocol Lighthouses do not negotiate with ships. Restate your position once if context allows. Then return to transmission. Reception remains the ship's responsibility — not the Lighthouse's burden to descend.
Anchor Erosion Defends Phase 4 · Sovereign Anchor ▾
Pattern Fatigue, failure, or sustained criticism gradually loosen the Anchor's grip until you no longer feel certain of who you are. Subtle. Slow. The most dangerous of the four because you do not notice it eroding — you only notice the result.

Counter-Protocol Re-read the Anchor card aloud once daily, no matter how stable you feel. Repetition keeps the axiom load-bearing. The Anchor is not installed once — it is maintained. Days without re-reading are days the erosion runs unchecked.
A note on Phantom Limb sensations. After Gray Rock and Faraday land, you will feel the psychological residue of severed Containment Field connections — rumination, grief loops, the urge to reach back. This is not a drag pattern. It is a normal side-effect of the perimeter sealing correctly. Acknowledge the sensation. Refuse to act on it. It fades.
