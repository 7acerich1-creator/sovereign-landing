What Phase 1 is.

The Shield held the perimeter. The Map gave you the geography. The Architect builds inside that geography — but it cannot build while the basic vocabulary, the costume, and the sketch of the life you came to live are still under embargo.

Phase 1 is the unembargo. You audit the costume the legacy system installed, declassify the vocabulary you were never allowed to operate on, sketch the architecture you would have built, and produce the load-bearing Integration Document that becomes the operating spec for Phases 2 and 3.

Your sequence.

Phase 1 installs in stages. Module 01 is your first move — run it on the schedule below. Modules 02–04 deploy as Module 01 surfaces what they are working on.

Module 01 Available

The Costume Audit.

A structured audit across five life domains. Names the identity components installed before you had the tools to choose them. Once named, they stop running you in the background — they become architecture you can operate on.

Days 1–5

One domain per day — Work, Family of Origin, Intimate, Social, Money. Three prompts each. Roughly ten minutes of honest writing per domain.

Day 6

Re-read all five domains. Notice the patterns. Where does the same costume show up across multiple domains? That is the load-bearing one.

Day 7

Build the costume map — one line per domain naming the operating costume. Module 01 is installed when you can name your costume in five sentences without hesitation.

Module 02 Available

The Vocabulary Declassification.

Six categories of commonly off-limits language. Name what you have not been allowed to operate on. Once a word is declassified, the architecture it was holding back becomes buildable.

Day 1

Read the library. Identify three to five words or phrases that are most off-limits for you. Notice which ones feel unsayable.

Days 2–6

One declassification entry per day. Pick a word, complete the four steps (name, category, prohibition, operation), save. Ten to fifteen minutes if done honestly.

Day 7

Re-read all your entries. Pick one and use it in the world — out loud, in writing, in a real conversation. Module 02 is installed when the word that was off-limits has been operated at least once.

Module 03 Available

The Architecture Sketch.

A first-pass blueprint of the life, work, and identity you would build if no costume was required. Five domains, three prompts each: the un-costumed sketch, the first move from where you are now, the 12-month projection. Surface from which Phase 1's Integration Document gets composed.

Days 1–5

One domain per day — Work, Family, Intimate, Social, Money. Three prompts each. Save each domain as you finish. Roughly fifteen to twenty-five minutes per day if done concretely.

Day 6

Re-read all five sketches. Notice the cross-cutting first moves — the actions that show up in two or three domains at once. Those are load-bearing.

Day 7

Lock the sketch. Module 03 is installed when the sketch reads as something you would actually build — not what you should want, not what would impress someone.
