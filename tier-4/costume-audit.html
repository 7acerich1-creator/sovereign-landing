Tier 4 · Phase 1 · Module 01

The Costume
Audit.

Five domains. Three prompts each. The identity components installed before you had the tools to choose them, named so they stop running you in the background and become architecture you can operate on.

What this installs.

The costume is not metaphor. It is the literal set of identity components — postures, voices, defaults, suppressions — installed by the legacy operating system before you had the language to refuse any of them. Most of what you call "your personality" is the costume. The audit names it. Once named, it stops being you and starts being something you can audit, edit, or take off.

How to use it.

One domain per day, in order. Roughly ten minutes of honest writing per domain. Save each one as you finish. After Day 5, all five domain costumes appear in the costume map below — that is your first declassified inventory. Re-edit any domain at any time as the audit refines.

Sovereign Tip

The ten minutes at the end of the day are the close-out. The real work is during the day — watching yourself, asking honestly why you are doing what you are doing. Every layer you uncover is a layer the architecture loses on you.

The deeper you go now, the fewer costumes you will have to peel back later. There is a level where you see all the way through and the system has no more power over you. That is sovereignty. This is the path. Use the Field Manual and Listening Companion below to push past the surface read — the depth you bring to these seven days is what compounds.

If the install slips during these seven days — and slips are a feature of the install, not a verdict — open the recovery manual for the named situations and the move that meets each one.

How to install Module 01.

A 7-day install. Days 1–5 are the audit itself. Days 6–7 are the synthesis that makes the audit usable.

Days 1–5

One domain per day, in order. Ten minutes of honest writing. Save each domain as you finish. Do not skip a day — the spacing is part of the install.

Day 6

Re-read all five domains. Notice the patterns. Where does the same costume show up across multiple domains? That is the load-bearing one.

Day 7

Look at the costume map. Module 01 is installed when you can name your costume across all five domains in a single sentence each, without hesitation. That single inventory is the surface from which Module 02 starts.

The costume was running you.
Naming it is how it stops.
