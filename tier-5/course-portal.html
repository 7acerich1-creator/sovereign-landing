What Phase 2 is.

Most architectures you walk through are not aligned with you. Some are mildly indifferent. Some are actively adversarial. Phase 2 is the systematic study of how to remain sovereign while inside a system that was built to capture you — without performing the costume the system rewards, and without the costly exit that most people default to.

Phase 2 builds on the Declassification work of Phase 1. It will not run in the absence of that foundation.

Curriculum modules.

Phase 2 is being deployed in waves to Tier 5 holders. As each module ships you will receive a notification by email and it will appear here.

  • 01 Adversarial Architecture Recognition The seven structural signatures of a system that is optimized to capture you. How to identify each one in the first interaction.
  • 02 The Non-Performance Posture How to remain present in an adversarial environment without producing the performance that the environment is optimized to extract.
  • 03 The Asymmetric Exit When exit is required, how to execute it on terms that preserve sovereignty rather than exhaust it.
  • 04 Phase 2 Capstone A live audit of one adversarial system you currently operate inside, with the specific Phase 2 moves applied.
